**DelhiVision**

**Ward-Wise Environmental Action Dashboard for Delhi**

**DelhiVision** is an integrated environmental intelligence platform that brings Air, Water and Land pollution data together — in one seamless dashboard, mapped ward-wise across Delhi. 
While existing systems focus primarily on air quality (AQI), DelhiVision expands the conversation by bringing water and land pollution into equal focus and linking data to actionable insights.

_✨ Designed for both citizens and government authorities to enable targeted, local environmental action._

**Key features:**

- Ward-wise pollution status for **air, water and land**. Focus on local problems, not just overall city metrics covering water and land pollution and not just air.

- Identification of **pollution hotspots and sources**

- **Government actions and mitigation measures**.Includes policy measures and advisories

- **Public advisories** for awareness and safety

- All integrated into a **single, intuitive interface**

**System Overview**

- Data collection from sensors, reports and govenrment reports

- Ward-wise processing and pollution analysis

- Hotspot detection and recommendations

- Interactive dashboard visualization

**Technologies Used**

- **Frontend:** React, Vite, Tailwind CSS

- **Mapping:** Leaflet, GeoJSON

- **Backend:** Node.js
